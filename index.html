HOW IT WORKS / 01—05

Receive the signal.

InboxSurvey reads the message headers and visible content you already have in Gmail. Nothing is copied to a remote inbox.

Prepare locally.

The local language model gets ready in your browser. Your mailbox stays where it is while the scan is prepared.

Protect the details.

Passwords, one-time codes, payment details, and identity numbers are removed before a message is interpreted.

Read the pattern.

Each message is examined for subscriptions, newsletters, system updates, useful mail, and suspicious signals.

Return a clear field.

Your results are organized into a simple private ledger so you can see what deserves attention without opening every email.

PRIVACY, WITHOUT THE ASTERISK

The work happens on your device.

InboxSurvey requests Gmail read-only access, then loads its classifier in your browser. It does not need permission to send, edit, or delete email.

Credentials, IDs, financial values, and URL tokens are redacted before analysis.
You can revoke Gmail access from your Google account at any time.
